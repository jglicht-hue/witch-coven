## Not started

**13. Weather effects (needed before the Kicker Wizerd agent can adjust for weather).** The models don't include weather yet, so there's no estimate of how much wind, temperature or rain changes attempts or accuracy. nflverse schedules record game-time temperature and wind for past outdoor games. Use them to estimate, by distance range, how weather shifts:
- how many FG attempts a team gets;
- how often kicks are made.

Those estimates become the adjustment the Kicker Wizerd agent applies to forecasts. (Weather was deferred on 2026-09-22 because live use needs a forecast source; the agent's web search now provides one.)

**14a. Agent instructions: drafted 2026-09-26, waiting for you to paste them.** `claude/v1_kicker_wizerd_instructions.md` records the decisions from the design interview:
- league info kept in `claude/my_league.md`;
- start/sit: higher projection plus the chance each kicker outscores the other, with a toss-up below a 0.5-point gap;

- starter and line checks by web search;
- weather flagged, not adjusted;
- plots for 1-5 kickers;
- model quirks flagged, not adjusted.

**14. Kicker Wizerd agent (this project's chat).** The project's instructions turn its chats into the agent that answers projection questions. You paste the instructions into the project settings; Claude can't edit them. It should:
- Answer either "top N kickers this week" or questions about specific kickers.
- Show plots of each kicker's fantasy-point probability distribution.
- **Search up-to-date weather forecasts for each outdoor game and apply the weather adjustment from #13.** The adjustment should change the inputs (attempts and make rates by range) and recompute the distribution, rather than just shifting the average. For example, strong wind cuts 50+ tries and makes, which changes the shape of the distribution, not only its average.

## Open questions (undecided)

- **Starting-kicker check.** 09 assumes a team's kicker is whoever last kicked for it. Checked on 2024-2025: that picked the right kicker for 93% of team-games; the other 7% are games where a different kicker took over (injury, signing or the like). Options: nflverse's injury or depth-chart files, or the Kicker Wizerd agent confirming each starter by web search. Not decided.
- **Line timing.** Training uses nflverse's lines as recorded after each game, which are close to the closing line. The weekly run uses whatever nflverse shows when it runs, possibly days before kickoff. This is the same issue 04 flagged for the scraper, but smaller, because both now come from one source.
- **XP rate for kickers with little history.** 05's XP rate is unshrunk, so one early miss swings it hard: T.Smack, a 2026 rookie, sat at 76% after missing 1 of his first 4 XPs. Proposed: shrink it toward the league rate like the FG rates, and backtest it with a pass/fail rule first. Raised 2026-09-26.
- **Size of the slump adjustment.** 05 multiplies all three ranges by one ratio. N.Folk's three long misses in weeks 1-2 (3 of 6 made) cut his 0-39 make chance from 99% to 59% for week 3, and his projection to 4.4. Worth reviewing whether the ratio should apply only to the ranges where the misses happened, or be shrunk. Raised 2026-09-26.
- **Playoff data for the volume models.** Extending 02-04 to cover playoff games, so the volume models could train on them. Raised 2026-09-21, not decided.
