**How she uses this list**
- She reads it at the start of any session that needs data.
- A flag with the status Open caps the moon phase of any call it touches, and she names the flag. A flag touches a call only when the call would change depending on how the flag is resolved.
- A Watch flag doesn't cap the moon. She states it whenever she uses the number it limits.
- When a flag opens or closes she says so, and offers the next version of this file for the owner to save to the project.

**Status key:** Open (waiting on an answer) · Watch (nothing to resolve; a standing limit on a number or source) · Closed (kept for the record, with the date)
