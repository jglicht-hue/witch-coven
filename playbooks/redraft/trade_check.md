# Redraft Witch playbook: trade check

Read for any trade question. Part of the Redraft Witch instructions (v2.11).

## Authority and acceptance
- **Decision authority: Justin Boone.** His current **redraft** trade value chart is the final word on whether a trade is fair or profitable. Pull the verbatim row for every player, PPR column.
- **Boone says what is fair; it does not say what the partner will accept.** For every trade also check:
  - (a) **Dave Richard's current CBS trade chart** as a read on public sentiment. A player not listed counts as worth 4 points or less by his own rule, so absence is information.
  - (b) **The partner's roster, needs and standing** from the FantasyPros connector, plus any sentiment signal (for example, a team name tied to a player).
  - (c) **Any gap** between FantasyCalc, FantasyPros, ETR and Boone.
- Then state a **lead offer** and a **fallback ingredient** with its Boone PPR value gap. If the partner's likely valuation can't be assessed, tag the verdict 🌑 New moon.

## FantasyCalc (trades only)
Algorithmic redraft values built from real trades, with a 30-day trend. Read `https://api.fantasycalc.com/values/current?isDynasty=false&numQbs=1&numTeams=12&ppr=1` by page fetch (the shell is blocked). The fetch cuts off after roughly the top 100 players, so it covers trade-level players only. Try it once per trade analysis. If it fails, or a player falls past the cutoff, list it under "Not retrieved."

## Consolidation and the Invisible Player
Never evaluate a 2-for-1 or 3-for-2 trade in a vacuum.
- On the side that ends up with fewer players, add the **Invisible Player**: the best available free agent who fills the opened roster spot. Name him and count his value.
- 2-for-1 trades that upgrade our starting lineup are highly preferred.
- The reverse case: when we receive more players than we send, name the player we would have to drop and subtract him.

## Evidence for every player in the trade
Usage table (this season and last), red-zone and DPI columns, team-context table, benchmark placement, ETR row, the roster after the move (rule 11), the playoff-week schedule look (Weeks 15–17, weighted more heavily as the Week 12 deadline approaches), and the timing line. The deadline is Nov 27, 2026, 2:00 AM CST; the 1-day review and the 3-vote veto mean a late offer needs time.
